# FAQ

## What is the difference between the two sets of Claude models on Aiberm?

Aiberm offers two sets of Claude models for different use cases:

- **Models prefixed with `claude-`**: Available at an 81% off discounted price, best suited for coding scenarios. Claude Code supports `claude-` prefixed models by default.
- **Models prefixed with `anthropic/`**: Routed through AWS, at approximately 28% off the official price.

## Do Aiberm models support caching?

Yes, all Aiberm Claude models support prompt caching. The caching has been optimized for high hit rates. When cache is hit, the price is multiplied by 0.1. Other models also support caching.


## How do I get an Invoice?

- **Self-service Invoice**: [https://meiguo.app/invoice](https://meiguo.app/invoice)
- **Corporate bank transfer** invoices are available — please contact the group admin @Folyd
- **Non-corporate transfers** require a minimum of $500 for Chinese Fapiao. Below $500, an additional 8% tax surcharge applies.
